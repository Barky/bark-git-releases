Bark Git

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

Bark Git is a desktop app that runs on your Mac. It has no user accounts and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your Mac

Your repositories, code, commit history, settings, and the API keys and access tokens you add (stored in the macOS Keychain) stay on your Mac. We never receive them.

License server

To activate a license or run the trial, the app sends our license server your license key and a one-way hash of your Mac's hardware identifier. The hash lets us bind a license to one Mac without learning the identifier itself. We store the key, the hash, and the activation or trial start time. The server runs on Cloudflare.

AI providers

If you use AI features, the app sends the relevant code changes, branch name, and recent commit subjects directly from your Mac to the AI provider you chose, using your own API key. This data does not pass through us. The app redacts common secrets before sending and shows you what will be sent. The provider's own privacy policy applies.

GitHub, Bitbucket, and Linear

If you connect these services, the app talks to them directly with the token you provide. That data does not pass through us.

Updates

The app checks for updates by downloading a feed file from GitHub. GitHub sees your IP address when this happens, as with any download.

Diagnostic reports

Diagnostic reports are off by default. A report is only sent if you turn the option on and send one yourself.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy. We receive your name, email address, and order details from Lemon Squeezy so we can support your license. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by email. Deleting license records means the license can no longer be restored on your Mac.
